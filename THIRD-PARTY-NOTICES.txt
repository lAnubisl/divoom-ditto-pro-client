BLE protocol implementation adapted from https://github.com/orionparrott/divoom-protocol

Linux D-Bus dependency: Tmds.DBus 0.95.1 (MIT)
https://github.com/tmds/Tmds.DBus
The Linux transport calls the documented BlueZ D-Bus API; BlueZ runs on the host.

Portable media decoding dependency: StbImageSharp 2.30.16
https://github.com/StbSharp/StbImageSharp
https://www.nuget.org/packages/StbImageSharp/2.30.16
